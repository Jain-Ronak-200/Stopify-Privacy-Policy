3. REQUIRED PERMISSIONS AND API USAGE

To function as intended, the Application requires specific device permissions. We strictly adhere to Google Play Developer Policies regarding the use of these permissions:

a. UsageStats API

The Application requires the "Usage Access" permission to retrieve data regarding the time you spend on various applications. This data is used exclusively on your device to calculate your screen time and populate your activity dashboard. It is never transmitted off your device.

b. Unrestricted Battery Usage

To accurately monitor screen time continuously in the background, the Application requires exemption from battery optimization.

c. Notifications

The Application requires notification permissions to alert you when you are approaching or have reached your screen time limits.

d. Display Over Other Apps (Overlay)

The Application requires the "System Alert Window" permission to display a blocking interface or warning overlay on top of other applications. This is a mechanical necessity to enforce your self-imposed screen time limits. This permission is not used to monitor or collect data from other apps.
